Important scope
---------------
- Stable dual-switch is enabled for Pro, Legit, and Forbidden AI.
- Normal and Hard AI retain conventional weapon switching.
- Utility weapons and launchers are excluded.
- A switch is armed only after authoritative shot confirmation.
- The newly equipped weapon must complete a confirmed shot before the AI may switch back.
- Local weapon snapshots without authoritative cooldown fields cannot create false shot confirmations.

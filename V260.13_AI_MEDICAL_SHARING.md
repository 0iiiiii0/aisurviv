# V260.13 AI Medical Sharing

## Purpose
Adds team medical-supply sharing for SmartBot AI, modeled after the existing AI ammo-sharing system.

## Behaviour
- Human teammates can request medicine with the existing `emote_medical` team emote.
- AI teammates automatically report medical need when injured and their own healing inventory is insufficient.
- Only team modes that already permit ammo sharing participate; Solo and Duel remain unaffected.
- One donor AI is reserved per request to prevent duplicate drops.
- Human requests are prioritised where the existing mode policy prioritises human ammo requests.
- Donors approach the requester, stop within roughly 5.2 world units, then use the normal authoritative DropItem path.
- Donors refuse/interrupt supply runs when outside gas, under airstrike danger, healing, reviving, in another special action, recently damaged, too injured, or an enemy is too close.
- Donors keep reserves: routine bandage reserve 3, emergency-human reserve 2; the last healthkit/soda/painkiller is never donated.
- Recipient health controls item preference: healthkit/bandage for serious injury; bandage first for moderate injury; boosts are fallback for healthier requesters.
- A successful gift clears the request and adds a 12-second served cooldown.

## Authoritative teammate-only drop
`Player.dropItem()` now supports teammate reservation for heal/boost items using the same server-only recipient validation pattern as AI ammo gifts.
- Only `serverBot` donors may create a recipient-locked supply drop.
- Recipient must be alive, connected, non-spectator, and in the same group or faction.
- Valid medicine gifts are locked to the intended teammate for 15 seconds.
- Invalid/cross-team recipient IDs degrade to a normal public drop.
- Human clients cannot spoof a recipient-locked drop because recipient validation rejects non-serverBot donors.
